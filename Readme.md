DA120A_Sales Performance Analytics

1. Project Title

Sales Performance Analysis

2. Brief Introduction

In today’s competitive retail and commercial landscape, organizations
generate large volumes of transactional data across multiple regions,
channels, and product categories. Understanding how products, customer
segments, and sales channels perform over time is essential for
sustainable revenue growth. This project examines historical sales
records to identify key revenue drivers, seasonal trends, and
operational bottlenecks, supporting data-driven commercial
decision-making.

3. Problem Statement

Despite having access to extensive historical sales records, many
businesses struggle to identify which products, regions, or customer
segments are driving profitability and which are reducing margins.
Fragmented reporting can lead to missed revenue opportunities,
inefficient inventory allocation, and inconsistent forecasting. There is
therefore a need to examine sales data systematically and understand the
factors affecting overall revenue performance.

4. Project Objectives

-   Analyze historical sales performance.
-   Identify revenue drivers and seasonal trends.
-   Evaluate product and regional sales performance.
-   Clean and integrate multiple datasets.
-   Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA).
-   Apply statistical hypothesis testing.
-   Generate meaningful business insights.

5. Project Scope

The project will focus on sales transactions, customer behavior, product
performance, regional sales, and revenue trends. Multiple datasets will
be collected, cleaned, integrated, and analyzed to identify meaningful
business patterns.

Transactions Dataset - Customer Information Dataset - Product

These are planned dataset categories, not yet-confirmed dataset sources.

9 Data Cleaning and Integration

The collected datasets will be examined and processed to: - Handle
missing values. - Identify and remove or appropriately manage duplicate
records. - Standardize inconsistent formats. - Correct data quality
issues. - Integrate datasets using common identifiers such as Customer
ID, Product ID, and Order ID, where available. - Record cleaning
decisions and data-quality issues.

10. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

EDA will be performed to investigate: - Sales and revenue trends. -
Product performance. - Regional sales patterns. - Customer purchasing
behavior. - Seasonal variations. - Sales and profit patterns, where the
required fields are available.

11. Hypothesis Testing

Statistical hypothesis testing will be used to investigate differences
and relationships in sales performance. Proposed questions include: - Do
discounted products generate higher sales than products without
discounts? - Does sales performance differ between regions? - Is there a
significant difference in sales between customer segments?

The final hypotheses and statistical tests will be selected after
inspecting the available data and checking the assumptions required by
each test.
